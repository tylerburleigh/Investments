# Energy Transition Thesis

> Themes within this thesis: [[strategy/theme-nuclear|Nuclear]], [[strategy/theme-ev|EV]]

## Thesis

Three converging forces: renewable penetration requiring storage to manage intermittency, AI/data center power demand creating structural scarcity for dispatchable power, and transportation electrification driving battery demand at scale. The theme is expressed across the value chain: next-gen battery technology (AMPX), upstream lithium supply (ELVR), and power generation with grid-scale storage (VST).

## Variant View

- **Market view**: electrification is consensus, but many battery/lithium equities are cyclical or structurally commoditized.
- **My view**: the investable edge is in bottlenecks and scarcity: dispatchable power for AI load, North American lithium leverage, and battery technologies that prove commercial adoption.
- **Why this matters**: VST can be treated as a quality anchor, while ELVR and AMPX need commodity/contract discipline rather than open-ended thematic conviction.

> [!analysis] I am not anchoring this thesis on a single global storage TAM.
> Definitions vary across total energy storage, grid-scale battery storage, and battery-system revenue, so the thesis rests on observed deployment growth and tightening power markets rather than a vendor market-size forecast.

## Secular Tailwinds

1. **Grid storage demand** — renewable penetration requires storage, and utility-scale deployment is still accelerating
2. **AI/data center power** — 24/7 dispatchable power creating baseload scarcity premium
3. **EV battery demand** — transportation electrification driving lithium demand and battery innovation
4. **Policy support** — IRA production/investment tax credits; state-level storage mandates
5. **Battery cost declines** — lithium-ion costs down 85%+ since 2010
6. **Supply chain reshoring** — Western push to reduce Chinese battery supply chain dependence

## Major Risks

- **Lithium oversupply (2027-2028)** — massive greenfield supply could flood market
- **Policy reversal** — IRA repeal would remove key incentives
- **Technology disruption** — sodium-ion or solid-state could commoditize lithium-ion faster than expected
- **Chinese competition** — CATL, BYD have massive cost advantages
- **Interest rate sensitivity** — capital-intensive infrastructure is rate-sensitive

## Key Position Details

**VST**: The quality anchor of the thesis and the cleanest listed way to express "AI power demand meets dispatchable generation." The main tension is valuation: the multiple only holds up if new power deals keep arriving.

**ELVR**: Pure commodity-cycle leverage to lithium through a North American asset base. This is not a compounder; it is a cyclical bet that should be sized and trimmed like one.

**AMPX**: Technology option on silicon-anode batteries. The upside case is broader commercial adoption; the downside case is that it remains a niche aerospace-and-defense story with too much valuation already embedded.
