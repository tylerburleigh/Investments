# Nuclear Energy Theme

> Theme within: [[strategy/thesis-energy-transition]]

## Thesis

Nuclear is experiencing a structural renaissance driven by AI power demand, grid decarbonization, and energy sovereignty. Data centers need 24/7 baseload power that only nuclear provides cleanly. The theme spans the full nuclear value chain: uranium production (CCO.TO), nuclear energy broadly (NLR ETF), and next-gen SMRs (OKLO). The key tension: the thesis is compelling but valuations already reflect significant optimism.

## Variant View

- **Market view**: the nuclear renaissance is increasingly consensus, and valuations already discount many positive policy headlines.
- **My view**: the best expression is a quality/commodity anchor plus a capped SMR option, not an open-ended bet that every nuclear story works.
- **Why this matters**: CCO.TO/NLR should be sized by uranium and services evidence; OKLO should stay capped until regulatory and fuel milestones improve.

## Secular Tailwinds

1. **AI power demand** — data centers need 24/7 dispatchable baseload; nuclear is the only clean option
2. **Government support** — US DOE pilot programs, UK "golden age" (£2.5B SMR investment), India 100 GW target
3. **Uranium supply deficit** — projected 197M lb deficit by 2040; long-term contracting prices rising ($93/lb Mar 2026)
4. **Energy sovereignty** — nations prioritizing domestic energy security post-Ukraine
5. **Regulatory reform** — NRC modernizing licensing pathways; pro-nuclear bipartisan support

## Major Risks

- **SMR execution track record is poor** — only 3 SMRs operating globally; all had 300-700% cost overruns and 12-year timelines vs 3-4 year projections
- **HALEU fuel crisis** — 40 metric tons needed by end of decade, only ~1 ton produced domestically; almost all current supply from Russia
- **Regulatory bottleneck** — NRC process designed for large LWRs; novel reactor licensing still being established
- **Valuations rich** — CCO is expensive on current earnings and OKLO carries a rich pre-revenue valuation; much of the "renaissance" is already priced in
- **Faster alternatives** — natural gas, solar+storage, geothermal can meet data center demand on 2-3 year timelines vs nuclear's 10+

## Key Position Details

**CCO.TO**: The quality anchor of the theme. It combines real uranium production with nuclear-services exposure through Westinghouse, which is a much stronger mix than a pure uranium price trade.

**NLR**: The ETF widens exposure beyond uranium miners into utilities and manufacturers. The overlap with Cameco is real, but it still captures parts of the nuclear value chain that a single stock does not.

**OKLO**: Pure asymmetric SMR execution bet. If Aurora hits milestones and the regulatory path improves, the upside is obvious; if not, the pre-revenue valuation will compress hard.
