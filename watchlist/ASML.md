# Watchlist: ASML

## Why Watching

Only provider of EUV lithography equipment — the sole bottleneck for advanced-node chip manufacturing. Every AI chip from NVDA, AMD, custom ASICs (GOOG, META) must pass through ASML's tools at TSM/Samsung/Intel. True monopoly one step upstream of all existing semi positions. Would benefit from AI capex cycle regardless of which chipmaker wins.

Held back by: heavy existing semiconductor concentration (NVDA, AMD, TSM, MU, AMKR), tech conviction basket already underweight at 7.7% vs 10% target, and priority order is MSFT/NVDA first. Also carries China export control risk and EUR/USD currency exposure.

## Entry Criteria

- Tech conviction basket reaches ~9% (close to 10% target) — room for a new name
- MSFT/NVDA/META/GOOG closer to target weight
- Valuation pullback to a more attractive entry (currently premium multiple)
- Or: thesis change where upstream equipment monopoly looks more attractive than downstream chip competition
