# Watchlist: XE

## Why Watching

X-Energy is a direct fit for [[strategy/theme-nuclear]]: advanced small modular reactors, nuclear fuel, and AI/data-center power demand. It is especially relevant as a benchmark for [[holdings/OKLO]] because both are SMR execution bets, while [[holdings/CCO.TO]] and [[holdings/NLR]] are broader nuclear/uranium expressions.

This is not automatically a better bet than OKLO. The key question is whether X-Energy offers superior customer validation, regulatory progress, funding runway, and commercialization visibility at a reasonable valuation.

## Entry Criteria

- IPO pricing leaves room for execution risk rather than capitalizing SMR deployment as certain.
- Customer agreements are firm enough to matter, not just MOUs.
- Fuel/manufacturing path and regulatory milestones are clearer than the public SMR peer group.
- Cash runway can support development without immediate heavy dilution.
- Risk/reward is better than adding to existing nuclear exposure.
