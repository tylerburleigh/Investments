# Watchlist: SPACEX

## Why Watching

SpaceX/xAI is the most relevant potential IPO for the portfolio because it intersects two active theses: [[strategy/thesis-space]] and [[strategy/thesis-ai]]. SpaceX is the dominant launch and satellite-internet company, and a public listing could become either a better expression of the space thesis or a major competitive overhang for [[holdings/RKLB]], [[holdings/ASTS]], [[holdings/LUNR]], and [[holdings/MDA.TO]].

The reported xAI combination also makes this an AI infrastructure and model-platform candidate, but that increases the need to understand related-party structure, cash burn, compute capex, and governance before treating it as investable.

## Entry Criteria

- Public S-1/prospectus is available, including enough financial detail to separate launch, Starlink, and xAI economics.
- Use of proceeds, capex needs, debt, and related-party transactions are understandable.
- Valuation does not require Starlink, launch, and xAI all compounding perfectly at once.
- Governance and control structure are acceptable for a long-term public holding.
- First-week trading does not leave the stock priced for perfection without a margin of safety.
