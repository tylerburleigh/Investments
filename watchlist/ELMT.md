# Watchlist: ELMT

## Why Watching

The Elmet Group makes critical materials and high-power microwave products used across aerospace, defense, semiconductor, medical, industrial, and energy markets. It is not a direct current thesis holding, but it is adjacent to several portfolio interests: defense/space supply chains, semiconductor manufacturing, and U.S. critical-materials reshoring.

This is a lower-priority IPO candidate than SpaceX or X-Energy. It deserves tracking because it could be a small picks-and-shovels industrial, but only if the public financials show quality and liquidity.

## Entry Criteria

- Public float and trading liquidity are sufficient for a small thematic position.
- Revenue, margin, backlog, and customer concentration support a durable industrial thesis.
- The "sole-source U.S. producer" claim is backed by filings, contracts, and pricing power.
- Valuation does not already price in a full defense/reshoring cycle.
- Thesis fit is strong enough to compete with existing AI, space, or nuclear uses of capital.
