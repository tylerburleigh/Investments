# NVTS — Navitas Semiconductor

## Thesis

GaN/SiC power semiconductor pure play. NVIDIA design win (800V power board for MGX) is the entire bull case — if AI data centers adopt Navitas power delivery at scale, the revenue inflection could be dramatic. GlobalFoundries partnership provides manufacturing scale.

NVIDIA 800V power board design win for MGX, FY2025 ~$46M revenue (half of FY2024), 25% short interest.

**On probation.** Revenue is declining (FY2025 ~$46M, half of FY2024). Deeply unprofitable. 25% short interest. AI data center revenue is aspirational, not yet material. The NVIDIA design win is the only thing keeping this position — without revenue materialization, the thesis is narrative, not fundamentals.

If H3 in [[strategy/thesis-ai|AI thesis]] disconfirms (no >$10M AI data center revenue by Q4 2026), exit the position.

## Catalysts

- NVIDIA MGX power board production ramp and revenue recognition
- Additional AI data center design wins
- Revenue inflection toward profitability
- GlobalFoundries partnership delivering manufacturing scale

## Risks

- **Revenue declining** — FY2025 was half of FY2024; negative trajectory
- **Unprofitable** — burning cash with no clear path to profitability
- **Narrative risk** — AI data center revenue is aspirational; may never materialize
- **Short interest** — 25% short interest creates volatility and reflects skepticism
- **Competition** — Infineon, Texas Instruments dominate power semiconductors
