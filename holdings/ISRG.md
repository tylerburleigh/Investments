# ISRG — Intuitive Surgical

## Thesis

Surgical robotics near-monopoly. 10,700+ da Vinci systems installed globally with ~80% market share. Recurring revenue model — instruments and accessories generate annuity-like revenue on every procedure. $8B+ revenue, 70%+ gross margins. Part of the [[strategy/theme-robotics|Robotics]] theme.

11,106 da Vinci systems installed, ~80% market share, $10.1B revenue, 70%+ gross margins, da Vinci systems $1.5M+.

Expanding into new procedures (Ion bronchoscopy, SP single-port) to grow beyond core urology/gynecology. The installed base is the moat — surgeon training, hospital integration, and procedure data create switching costs competitors can't replicate.

Conviction 3 reflects position maturity, not business quality — this is a new position (Apr 2026) with limited cost basis. The business itself is arguably conviction 4 quality; the 3 will be revisited as the position builds.

## Catalysts

- Procedure volume growth ≥10% YoY (instrument/accessory revenue compounding)
- Ion bronchoscopy installations reaching ≥600 systems
- New procedure approvals expanding addressable market
- International expansion accelerating installed base growth

## Risks

- **Valuation** — 50-60x earnings is a premium for a medical device company
- **Competition** — Medtronic Hugo, J&J Ottava gaining regulatory approvals
- **Saturation** — core procedures (urology, gynecology) may be approaching installed base limits
- **Hospital capital budgets** — da Vinci systems are $1.5M+; budget constraints could slow growth
- **Regulatory** — medical device approvals are slow and uncertain
