# SYM — Symbotic

## Thesis

AI-powered warehouse robotics. $22.3B backlog (~decade of revenue). Walmart 400-center deployment + SoftBank GreenBox JV. First GAAP profit in Q1 FY2026 — the inflection from cash burn to profitability is happening. Part of the [[strategy/theme-robotics|Robotics]] theme.

$22.3B backlog, Walmart 400-center deployment, first GAAP profit Q1 FY2026, 182x forward P/E, 27% short interest.

The bull case is the backlog: $22.3B contracted gives multi-year revenue visibility most growth companies don't have. The bear case is customer concentration — Walmart + SoftBank = vast majority of revenue. Single-contract risk is extreme. New customers must materialize to de-risk the thesis. 182x forward P/E and 27% short interest reflect this tension.

## Catalysts

- New customer wins beyond Walmart and SoftBank
- Walmart deployment expansion to ≥50 additional centers in 2026
- Continued GAAP profitability (confirming Q1 wasn't a one-off)
- GreenBox JV revenue recognition ramping

## Risks

- **Customer concentration** — Walmart + SoftBank = vast majority; loss of either is catastrophic
- **Valuation** — 182x forward P/E leaves no room for execution misses
- **Short interest** — 27% short reflects genuine skepticism about the thesis
- **Long sales cycles** — enterprise warehouse deployments take years
- **Amazon competition** — Amazon Robotics is the elephant in the room
