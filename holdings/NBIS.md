# NBIS — Nebius Group

## Thesis

AI-centric GPU cloud. FY2025 revenue $530M (+351% YoY). Microsoft deal $17.4B (up to $19.4B with additional capacity options), Meta partnership $3B. Targeting 800MW–1GW capacity by YE2026.

FY2025 revenue $530M and Meta partnership $3B.
Microsoft deal base value $17.4B confirmed via CNBC/press release; $19.4B upside contingent on Microsoft exercising additional capacity options.
65x trailing P/S, $16–20B capex in 2026.

The bet is that GPU cloud demand exceeds supply for years and Nebius captures a meaningful slice as an independent provider outside the hyperscaler oligopoly. Part of the [[strategy/thesis-ai|AI Thesis]].

Yandex legacy concerns overhang the name. 65x trailing P/S is expensive but defensible if the revenue trajectory holds. Massive $16–20B capex in 2026 — execution risk on buildout is real.

## Catalysts

- Capacity ramp toward 800MW–1GW target
- New hyperscaler / enterprise customer announcements
- Revenue trajectory confirming 300%+ growth is sustainable
- Yandex legacy resolution improving sentiment

## Risks

- **Yandex overhang** — legacy Russian business creates governance/sentiment drag
- **Dilution** — massive capex may require equity raises
- **Customer concentration** — Microsoft and Meta deals are huge but concentrated
- **Competition** — CoreWeave, Lambda, hyperscaler in-house options
- **Valuation** — 65x P/S leaves no room for deceleration
