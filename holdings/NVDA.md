# NVDA — NVIDIA Corp

## Thesis

GPU compute leader. CUDA ecosystem is the deepest moat in tech — the de facto standard for parallel computing across AI, scientific computing, and graphics. NVIDIA remains the primary beneficiary of each AI capex wave.

In Q4 FY2026, Data Center revenue was up 75% YoY. The bet is that CUDA lock-in and the pace of architecture transitions (Hopper → Blackwell → Rubin) keep NVIDIA multiple generations ahead of challengers. The stock trades at premium multiples, but growth and ecosystem lock-in still justify that premium if AI demand sustains.

Separate from [[strategy/thesis-ai|AI thesis]] infrastructure bets — we'd own NVDA regardless of the AI capex cycle, but it's the most AI-sensitive of the conviction names. First to trim in a serious downturn (see [[strategy/thesis-tech-conviction|Tech Conviction]] caveat).

## Catalysts

- Next-gen GPU architecture launches (Rubin timeline)
- Expanding inference demand as AI models deploy at scale
- Sovereign AI infrastructure buildouts globally
- Automotive and robotics revenue streams maturing

## Risks

- **Valuation stretch** — premium multiples; any growth deceleration triggers sharp repricing
- **AMD competition** — MI300x/MI400 gaining share (see H1 in thesis doc)
- **AI bubble contagion** — serious AI capex pullback hits NVDA first and hardest
- **Export controls** — China restrictions limit addressable market
- **Customer concentration** — hyperscaler capex decisions directly impact revenue
