# LI — Li Auto

## Thesis

Li Auto pioneered EREV (extended-range EV) technology in China — a small combustion engine acts as a generator, eliminating range anxiety. The core bet is that Li Auto's large cash position buys enough time to execute a turnaround after a brutal FY2025. The L-series refresh (L9 in Q2 2026) and Li i6 BEV ramp are near-term catalysts.

FY2025 revenue was US$16.1B with 406,343 deliveries. Li Auto reported US$14.5B of cash and cash equivalents, restricted cash, time deposits, and short-term investments at year-end. Q1 2026 deliveries reached 95,142, and Li i6 monthly deliveries surpassed 24,000 units in March 2026.

See [[strategy/theme-ev]] for full theme thesis.

## Catalysts

- L9 flagship refresh launching Q2 2026
- Li i6 BEV surpassing 24,000 monthly deliveries by March 2026
- Store Partner Program launching, expected to improve sales from Q3 2026
- MindVLA autonomous driving model (unveiled NVIDIA GTC March 2026)
- Company targeting 20% YoY delivery growth (~488K units) in 2026

## Risks

- EREV moat eroding: XPeng, BYD, Leapmotor all adding EREV variants
- Intelligent driving perception lag vs Huawei and XPeng
- Margin pressure from competitive pricing and product mix shift to lower-margin BEVs
- MEGA recall in Q3 2025 was a quality-control red flag
- Q1 2026 beat guidance but YoY growth was only 2.5%
