# INFQ — Infleqtion

## Thesis

Neutral-atom quantum computing + sensing. The scalability leader — neutral atom arrays can be scaled to thousands of qubits more easily than trapped ion. SPAC listing Feb 2026. Part of the [[strategy/theme-quantum|Quantum]] theme.

~2/3 of FY2025 revenue ($32.5M, +12.6%) from sensing, SPAC listing Feb 2026, 109x P/S, $516M cash, Beta 3.42, FY2024 revenue +164%.

Key tension: ~2/3 of FY2025 revenue ($32.5M, +12.6%) is from quantum *sensing*, not computing. INFQ's quantum computing thesis requires computing revenue to become the growth driver, but sensing is currently what pays the bills. If computing revenue doesn't ramp, INFQ is a sensing company at a quantum computing valuation (109x P/S).

NASA contracts (ISS hardware, gravity gradiometer) and NVIDIA partnership for materials science provide optionality. $516M cash. Highest beta in portfolio (3.42).

## Catalysts

- Computing revenue growth and mix shift away from sensing dominance
- NVIDIA partnership delivering commercial applications
- Neutral-atom qubit scaling milestones
- NASA contract milestones and new government wins

## Risks

- **Revenue composition** — sensing revenue ≠ computing thesis; valuations don't match
- **Growth deceleration** — 12.6% YoY in FY2025 vs 164% in FY2024
- **Valuation** — 109x P/S is the most expensive position in the portfolio
- **Beta** — 3.42x; extreme volatility
- **SPAC overhang** — SPAC listings often underperform long-term
- **Competition** — QuEra and Pasqal also pursuing neutral atom
