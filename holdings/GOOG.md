# GOOG — Alphabet Inc

## Thesis

Search advertising cash machine. Google Search is the most profitable monopoly in tech — default status across browsers and devices creates a distribution moat no competitor can replicate. Cloud growing 30%+. DeepMind is elite AI research. YouTube undermonetized relative to its reach.

Valuation remains reasonable relative to the cash flow profile, growth rate, and optionality. Would hold through normal cycles. The main risk is regulatory — DOJ antitrust case could impose remedies, but forced breakup is unlikely and even operational restrictions would take years to implement (see H2 in [[strategy/thesis-tech-conviction|Tech Conviction]]).

## Catalysts

- AI overviews / search generative experience driving engagement
- Cloud growth accelerating (30%+)
- YouTube monetization improvements (Shorts, Connected TV)
- Waymo optionality as autonomous ride-hailing scales
- DeepMind breakthroughs feeding into products

## Risks

- **DOJ antitrust** — remedies phase pending; forced divestiture or operational restrictions possible
- **AI disruption to search** — if LLMs replace search as the default information interface
- **Ad market cyclicality** — revenue tied to advertising budgets
- **Cloud market share** — GCP remains #3 behind AWS and Azure
