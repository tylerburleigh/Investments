# ELVR — Sayona Mining (Piedmont Lithium)

## Thesis

North America's largest independent hard-rock lithium producer (NAL mine, Quebec). Spodumene prices surging above $2,000/t in Q1 2026. Piedmont merger simplified ownership. Up 232% over 12 months. Part of the [[strategy/thesis-energy-transition|Energy Transition]] thesis.

"North America's largest independent hard-rock lithium producer", spodumene prices above $2,000/t in Q1 2026, up 232% over 12 months.

This is a commodity cycle bet that has already worked, not a compounding quality position. Cyclical peak risk is real — 2027–2028 lithium oversupply could flood the market. Do not add on strength. Trim if spodumene drops below $1,500/t.

## Catalysts

- Spodumene price continuation above $2,000/t
- NAL mine production expansion
- Piedmont merger synergies and cost reductions
- US domestic lithium supply chain policy support

## Risks

- **Cyclical peak** — up 232% in 12 months; commodity cycles reverse violently
- **Oversupply 2027–2028** — massive greenfield lithium supply coming online
- **Chinese competition** — CATL and others have massive cost advantages
- **IRA policy risk** — tax credit repeal would remove domestic advantage
- **Commodity pricing** — spodumene is uncorrelated to company execution
