The quality anchor of the [[strategy/theme-nuclear|nuclear theme]] — profitable, growing, with a unique uranium production + nuclear services combination.

- Uranium spot price appreciation (supply deficit thesis)

- **Valuation** — 37x EV/EBITDA prices in substantial uranium price appreciation
- **Account constraint** — Canadian-listed, RRSP only; limits flexibility
- **Uranium spot volatility** — commodity pricing drives earnings
- **Production execution** — McArthur River/Key Lake ramp-up risks
- **Oversupply** — if uranium supply deficit thesis doesn't materialize, contract book is worth less
