# MSFT — Microsoft Corporation

## Thesis

Enterprise lock-in via Office/Azure/Windows. The deepest recurring revenue model in tech — hundreds of millions of businesses depend on Microsoft's stack daily. Copilot is embedding AI across the entire product suite, turning AI capex into incremental SaaS revenue rather than speculative bets.

Most defensive of the [[strategy/thesis-tech-conviction|Tech Conviction]] names. Revenue is least exposed to AI cycle volatility because the base business (Office, Windows, enterprise agreements) is contractual and sticky. Even in a serious AI pullback, MSFT's core barely flinches. The premium multiple is justified by quality and defensibility. First priority for new capital within tech conviction.

## Catalysts

- Copilot adoption accelerating across Office 365 enterprise base
- Azure taking share from AWS (especially for AI workloads)
- GitHub Copilot as developer tooling monetization
- Gaming (Activision integration) as incremental optionality

## Risks

- **Valuation** — premium multiple leaves limited margin of safety
- **Azure competition** — AWS and GCP investing aggressively
- **AI capex drag** — massive infrastructure spending with uncertain long-term ROI
- **Antitrust** — less acute than GOOG/META but cloud regulation is emerging
