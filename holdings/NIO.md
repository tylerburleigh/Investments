# NIO — Chinese Premium EV

## Thesis

NIO is a bet on the premium end of Chinese EV manufacturing. The differentiator is battery swap technology (3,790+ stations worldwide) and a premium brand identity — closer to a Chinese Tesla than a budget EV maker. The thesis depends on NIO achieving profitability before cash runs out. Sub-brands (ONVO, Firefly) expand the addressable market but add execution complexity.

> [!source] Battery swap station count 3,790+ confirmed via [NIO press release](https://www.nio.com/news/20260206001) (Feb 2026).
FY2025 revenue reached RMB87.5B (US$12.5B), year-end cash and investments were RMB45.9B (US$6.6B), and Q1 2026 deliveries reached 83,465 (+98.3% YoY, above guidance).

See [[strategy/theme-ev]] for full theme thesis.

## Catalysts

- ONVO and Firefly sub-brand ramp driving volume growth
- Battery swap network reaching critical density in China
- European expansion (if tariffs allow)
- Positive vehicle gross margin trajectory

## Risks

- Cash burn: rarely sustainably profitable; dependent on capital markets
- Competitive intensity: BYD, XPeng, Li Auto, Xiaomi, Huawei all competing for share
- Battery swap may not scale cost-effectively vs fast-charging alternatives
- Tariffs blocking international expansion
- Dilution risk from ongoing capital raises
