# AMPX — Amprius Technologies

## Thesis

Silicon anode battery technology. 500 Wh/kg cells (2x conventional lithium-ion). FY2025 revenue $73M (3x YoY), approaching positive EBITDA. Nokia drone partnership, defense contracts. Part of the [[strategy/thesis-energy-transition|Energy Transition]] thesis.

500 Wh/kg cells (2x conventional), FY2025 revenue $73M (3x YoY), 39x P/S.

The bet is whether silicon anode breaks into automotive and grid storage or stays niche aerospace/defense. 39x P/S prices in the breakout case. If the technology remains defense-only, the thesis narrows significantly and conviction drops to 2.

## Catalysts

- First commercial (non-defense) contract — automotive or grid storage
- Positive EBITDA achievement
- Production scale-up milestones at Fremont facility
- Defense contract expansion and revenue recognition

## Risks

- **Technology lock-in** — if silicon anode stays niche, 39x P/S is unjustifiable
- **Competition** — CATL, Samsung SDI, solid-state alternatives
- **Production scaling** — lab performance ≠ manufacturing at scale
- **Customer concentration** — revenue concentrated in few defense contracts
- **Cash burn** — approaching EBITDA breakeven but not there yet
