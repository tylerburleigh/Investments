# LUNR — Intuitive Machines

## Thesis

First private company to soft-land on the Moon. $943M backlog (with Lanteris). Guided $900M–$1B revenue in 2026. Lunar infrastructure is a real market — NASA CLPS program funds multiple providers for cargo delivery, and LUNR is the only private company that has actually landed. Part of the [[strategy/thesis-space|Space Economy]] thesis.

> [!source] Lanteris acquisition confirmed — $800M deal closed Jan 13, 2026, formerly Maxar Space Systems. Combined backlog ~$943M confirmed via [Intuitive Machines 8-K](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/LUNR/8-k-intuitive-machines-inc-reports-material-event-6e423ce5879a.html).
$900M–$1B 2026 revenue guidance.

Binary execution risk: both prior landings tipped over. IM-3 must succeed. If it fails, lunar credibility is severely damaged and the stock could reprice -50%, though NASA CLPS may continue funding multiple providers. Position sizing reflects this binary risk.

## Catalysts

- IM-3 mission success (soft landing and payload delivery)
- Revenue ramp toward $900M–$1B guidance in 2026
- NASA CLPS contract renewals and new mission assignments
- Lunar infrastructure services (communication, navigation) monetization

## Risks

- **IM-3 binary risk** — two prior tip-overs; another failure is catastrophic for credibility
- **Customer concentration** — NASA is the primary customer
- **Revenue recognition timing** — lumpy, mission-dependent
- **Valuation** — priced for execution; any miss is punished sharply
