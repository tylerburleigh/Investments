# META — Meta Platforms

## Thesis

3B+ daily active users across Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp. Social graph is an unassailable moat — network effects at this scale are nearly impossible to replicate. AI-driven ad targeting is improving monetization quarter over quarter. Llama open-source strategy building AI credibility and developer ecosystem.

> [!source] FTC case dismissed November 18, 2025 — confirmed via [Sullivan & Cromwell memo](https://www.sullcrom.com/insights/memo/2025/December/Meta-Prevails-FTC-Monopolization-Case) and [Congress.gov LSB11379](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/LSB11379).

Zuckerberg executing well: Year of Efficiency cleaned up cost structure, AI investments are flowing directly into ad revenue uplift, and the moat remains obvious. Would hold through normal market cycles. A core position in [[strategy/thesis-tech-conviction|Tech Conviction]].

## Catalysts

- AI-driven ad targeting improvements flowing through to revenue
- Llama ecosystem growth strengthening developer moat
- Reels monetization continuing to mature
- Messaging (WhatsApp Business) as an under-monetized revenue stream

## Risks

- **Regulatory** — EU DMA enforcement ongoing; FTC case dismissed 2025 but scrutiny remains
- **AI bubble contagion** — ad spend contracts in a serious downturn
- **Reels/TikTok competition** — short-form video attention is contested
- **Reality Labs drag** — metaverse spending continues to burn cash
