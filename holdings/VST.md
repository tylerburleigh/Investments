Fortune 500 integrated power company. The "AI power demand" proxy — Meta 20-year PPA (2,609 MW nuclear), Amazon PPA. FY2025 adj. EBITDA $5.9B, guiding $6.8–7.6B in 2026. Investment-grade credit achieved. Generating >$10B cash through 2027.

Meta 20-year PPA for 2,609 MW nuclear, Amazon PPA details.
FY2025 adj. EBITDA $5.912B, 2026 guidance $6.8–7.6B, ">$10B cash through 2027", 68x P/E.

Core holding of the [[strategy/thesis-energy-transition|energy transition thesis]]. Multi-decade PPAs with hyperscalers provide contracted revenue visibility. The bet is that AI data centers need 24/7 dispatchable baseload power and VST's nuclear + gas fleet is perfectly positioned.

68x P/E is rich for a power company, justified only if AI data center PPAs continue compounding. If no new PPAs land in 2026, the multiple compresses.

## Catalysts

- New AI data center PPA announcements
- Nuclear capacity expansion or uprate
- Grid-scale storage deployment milestones
- Investment-grade credit opening institutional investor access

## Risks

- **Valuation** — 68x P/E assumes continued PPA growth; any deceleration compresses the multiple
- **Regulatory** — power generation is heavily regulated; rate case outcomes matter
- **Commodity exposure** — natural gas prices affect margins
- **Nuclear operational risk** — plant outages are high-impact events
