# BTC — Bitcoin

## Thesis

Structural long-term allocation. Bitcoin represents a fundamental evolution of the financial system — the transition to on-chain money and decentralized settlement. The bet is that the financial system itself is changing and BTC is foundational infrastructure for that transition.

28.6% of portfolio concentration.

**"Permanent" means the position is never fully exited, not that the allocation is never adjusted.** Value-averaging toward a target weight (~25% of portfolio, see [[strategy/Investment Strategy]]) is active: DCA pauses when overweight, resumes when underweight, and trimming at cycle tops is on the table if the 4-year halving cycle still holds. The core holding stays through drawdowns.

Spot ETFs, institutional adoption, and declining exchange supply are symptoms of this larger shift, not the thesis itself.

## Catalysts

- Continued spot ETF inflows from pensions, endowments, sovereign wealth
- Nation-state adoption ( sovereign reserve allocations)
- Exchange liquid supply declining (institutional "diamond handing")
- Regulatory clarity improving in US (pro-crypto administration)
- Potential as inflation hedge / "digital gold" narrative maturing

## Risks

- Macro headwinds: high rates limit upside momentum
- Regulatory reversal: policy shifts could crater institutional demand
- Concentration: 28.6% of portfolio in a single asset
- Volatility: 50%+ drawdowns are normal even in bull markets
- Technological: protocol-level issues, quantum computing threat long-term
- Narrative risk: if "digital gold" thesis doesn't hold, the floor drops
