4.5 GW secured power pipeline, GPU fleet scaling to 150K, Beta 4.3x, $6B ATM.

Higher risk/reward than [[holdings/APLD]] — IREN is an operating pivot with crypto legacy; execution risk is real. Massive dilution risk ($6B ATM). Beta 4.3x. The bet is that the power pipeline + Microsoft deal execution will create a multi-bagger, but the path is volatile.
