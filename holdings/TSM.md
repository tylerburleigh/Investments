# TSM — Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing

## Thesis

World's dominant chip foundry. Manufactures for NVDA, AMD, Apple, Qualcomm — essentially every leading-edge chip design company. Moat deepens with each node advancement as Intel and Samsung fall further behind on cadence and yields. N2 entered volume production on TSMC's 2025 roadmap, and A16 remains targeted for H2 2026. A [[strategy/thesis-tech-conviction|Tech Conviction]] holding.

Conviction 3 reflects geopolitical discount, not business quality. Would be conviction 4 without Taiwan risk — TSM's competitive position is actually stronger than some of the conviction 4 names. The discount is proportional to the existential nature of the risk. No hedge beyond position sizing.

## Catalysts

- A16 volume production (H2 2026) confirming continued node leadership
- N2 ramp and yield progression confirming the H2 2025 roadmap
- Advanced packaging (CoWoS) capacity expansion easing AI chip bottlenecks
- Intel foundry struggles deepening TSM's monopoly
- Automotive and HPC demand diversifying revenue

## Risks

- **Taiwan Strait** — existential geopolitical risk; no hedge beyond sizing
- **Earthquake / natural disaster** — concentrated manufacturing footprint
- **Customer concentration** — top customers (Apple, NVDA) have significant bargaining power
- **Currency** — TWD/USD fluctuations impact reported earnings
