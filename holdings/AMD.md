# AMD — Advanced Micro Devices

## Thesis

Challenger to NVDA in GPUs (MI300x/MI400) and Intel in data center CPUs. The thesis is independent, not just a NVDA hedge: AMD can win on cost/performance in data center CPUs (vs Intel) and capture enough GPU share to grow meaningfully even if NVDA dominates. Part of the [[strategy/thesis-tech-conviction|Tech Conviction]] bucket.

MI300 winning hyperscaler design slots.

Conviction 3 reflects execution risk of being #2 in both GPU and data center CPU. Market share gains are real — MI300 is winning hyperscaler design slots — but AMD hasn't yet proven it can sustain share against NVDA's architecture cadence. The bet is that the market is large enough for a strong #2.

## Catalysts

- MI400 architecture launch and hyperscaler adoption
- Data center CPU share gains vs Intel (EPYC continuing to take share)
- PC cycle recovery driving client revenue
- Custom silicon / embedded growth (Xilinx integration)

## Risks

- **Execution risk** — being #2 in both GPU and CPU means no margin for error
- **NVDA moat** — CUDA lock-in makes GPU share gains slow and expensive
- **Intel turnaround** — if Intel recovers, AMD's CPU share gains reverse
- **Valuation** — priced for continued share gains; miss = repricing
