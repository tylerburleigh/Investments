# IONQ — IonQ

## Thesis

Trapped-ion quantum computers with world-record 99.99% two-qubit gate fidelity — the highest accuracy of any qubit technology. FY2025 revenue $130M (+202% YoY). The commercialization leader in quantum computing. Part of the [[strategy/theme-quantum|Quantum]] theme.

> [!source] 99.99% two-qubit gate fidelity — confirmed via [IonQ press release](https://investors.ionq.com/news/news-details/2025/IonQ-Achieves-Landmark-Result-Setting-New-World-Record-in-Quantum-Computing-Performance/default.aspx) (Oct 21, 2025).
FY2025 revenue $130M (+202% YoY), SkyWater acquisition $1.8B pending, 256-qubit system Q4 2026, 10,000 qubits by 2027, $3.3B cash, $510M GAAP net loss, 71x P/S.

SkyWater acquisition ($1.8B pending) is the strategic bet — controlling the full stack (design + fabrication) and qualifying as a domestic supplier for government contracts. The risk is spending $4B+ on acquisitions before proving the core technology scales to thousands of logical qubits.

256-qubit system targeted Q4 2026, 10,000 qubits by 2027. $3.3B cash provides runway but $510M GAAP net loss shows how far profitability is. 71x P/S.

## Catalysts

- 256-qubit system demonstration (Q4 2026)

## Risks

- **Valuation** — 71x P/S prices in flawless execution
- **Acquisition integration** — $4B+ spent on M&A before proving core technology scales
- **Timeline uncertainty** — useful quantum advantage may be 15-30 years away (Jensen Huang)
- **Dilution** — aggressive M&A funded by equity
- **Winner-take-most** — 5+ qubit technologies; most won't survive independently
