# APLD — Applied Digital

## Thesis

AI data center builder/operator — more real-estate-like than [[holdings/IREN]]. CoreWeave 400 MW anchor tenant + new 15-year hyperscaler lease (~$5B). ~$16B contracted revenue provides long-term visibility. Potential REIT conversion would create a different return profile entirely. Part of the [[strategy/thesis-ai|AI Thesis]].

CoreWeave 400 MW anchor, 15-year hyperscaler lease ~$5B, ~$16B contracted revenue, $2.7B debt vs $319M TTM revenue.

The bet is that AI data center demand is so supply-constrained that APLD's pipeline of secured sites with power interconnection becomes extraordinarily valuable. Building and leasing capacity rather than operating GPU compute — infrastructure landlord, not operator.

$2.7B debt vs $319M TTM revenue is the tension. Leverage cuts both ways — it amplifies returns if execution holds, but the debt service is real.

## Catalysts

- Hyperscaler lease revenue recognition ramping
- REIT conversion announcement (would re-rate the valuation)
- New data center site energizing milestones
- Contracted revenue growing beyond $16B

## Risks

- **Leverage** — $2.7B debt vs $319M revenue is aggressive; execution must hold
- **Customer concentration** — CoreWeave and one hyperscaler dominate the book
- **Construction risk** — data center buildouts are capital-intensive and timeline-sensitive
- **Power interconnection** — delays in grid connection would push revenue recognition
- **Interest rate sensitivity** — heavy debt load magnifies rate risk
