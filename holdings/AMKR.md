# AMKR — Amkor Technology

## Thesis

World's largest US-headquartered OSAT (outsourced semiconductor assembly and test).

"World's largest US-headquartered OSAT". Advanced packaging (CoWoS, SoIC) is the primary bottleneck constraining AI chip supply — TSMC can't package chips fast enough. AMKR benefits as the leading independent packaging provider scaling to meet this structural shortage.

FY2025 revenue $6.71B (+6% YoY). $7B Arizona campus near TSMC fabs (production 2028). 45x trailing P/E — most reasonably valued in the [[strategy/thesis-ai|AI thesis]]. The advanced packaging bottleneck is structural, not cyclical — it takes years to build new packaging capacity.

FY2025 revenue $6.71B (+6% YoY), $7B Arizona campus, 45x trailing P/E.

## Catalysts

- TSMC CoWoS capacity expansion driving outsourced packaging demand
- Arizona campus construction milestones (production 2028)
- Advanced packaging revenue growing as mix shifts to higher-margin
- CHIPS Act benefits for domestic semiconductor manufacturing

## Risks

- **Customer concentration** — top customers include Apple, Qualcomm, NVDA
- **TSMC insourcing** — if TSMC builds enough packaging capacity internally, AMKR's role diminishes
- **Cyclicality** — semiconductor packaging is still cyclical even with structural tailwinds
- **Execution** — Arizona campus is a multi-year buildout; delays would hurt sentiment
