# NLR — VanEck Uranium+Nuclear Energy ETF

## Thesis

Broad nuclear/uranium ETF spanning miners, utilities, and equipment providers. Top holdings include CEG, CCJ, and BWXT. Provides exposure to nuclear utilities and manufacturers that individual uranium holdings don't capture. Part of the [[strategy/theme-nuclear|Nuclear]] theme.

Morningstar still rates the fund "Negative". VanEck's January 31, 2026 fact sheet showed a fund-level P/E of ~26x.

Held alongside [[holdings/CCO.TO]] because NLR captures nuclear utilities and manufacturers that CCO.TO doesn't. The overlap in miners is acceptable — it's overweight uranium inside a uranium bull thesis.

Conviction 3 reflects Morningstar's "Negative" rating and general ETF froth. The valuation is not as extreme as a triple-digit single-stock multiple, but the theme is still crowded.

## Catalysts

- Uranium spot price appreciation
- New nuclear PPA announcements from hyperscalers
- SMR regulatory progress (ADVANCE Act implementation)
- Global nuclear capacity expansion targets

## Risks

- **Overlap with CCO.TO** — miner exposure is duplicative; consider whether both are necessary
- **International exposure** — the fund includes non-North American and state-linked operators that add policy and governance risk
- **Morningstar "Negative"** — professional rating suggests overvaluation
- **~26x fund P/E** — reasonable versus the old triple-digit figure, but still elevated for a thematic ETF
- **Nuclear policy reversal** — government support could shift
