# MU — Micron Technology

## Thesis

Memory cyclical positioned here rather than in [[strategy/thesis-tech-conviction|Tech Conviction]] because the thesis is tied to the AI capex cycle, not durable platform compounding. HBM (high-bandwidth memory) demand from AI training and inference is the growth driver. The bet is on the memory upcycle coinciding with AI demand — if HBM pricing rolls over or AI capex pulls back, MU underperforms regardless of company quality.

Conviction 2 reflects cycle-aware sizing. This is a trade on memory cycle timing, not a long-term compounder. Watching to see if HBM revenue growth justifies a conviction upgrade or if the cycle turns.

## Catalysts

- HBM3e volume production ramp and customer qualification
- AI inference demand driving sustained memory bandwidth needs
- Memory upcycle continuing (pricing firmness)
- Data center revenue mix shifting toward higher-margin HBM

## Risks

- **Cycle risk** — memory is cyclical; pricing rolls over predictably
- **HBM competition** — SK Hynix and Samsung also scaling HBM capacity
- **AI capex pullback** — if AI buildout slows, HBM demand drops sharply
- **Commodity drag** — NAND and DRAM commodity segments are subject to oversupply
