# ETH — Ethereum

## Thesis

Programmable blockchain — the decentralized compute infrastructure layer. Smart contracts, DeFi, and L2 ecosystem make ETH the platform where most on-chain activity happens. The ETH thesis is distinct from [[holdings/BTC|BTC]]: BTC is money/settlement, ETH is decentralized compute. Part of the [[strategy/thesis-crypto|Crypto Thesis]].

Value accrual comes from gas fees on the world's most used smart contract platform + staking yield. L2 scaling (Arbitrum, Optimism, Base) is dual-edged: it grows the ecosystem but reduces L1 fee revenue. Conviction 3 (vs BTC at 4) reflects ETH's weaker monetary premium and ongoing questions about value accrual at scale.

DCA paused alongside BTC — crypto allocation at 37% vs 25% target.

## Catalysts

- L2 ecosystem growth driving overall network usage
- Staking yield attracting institutional holders post-ETH ETF
- DeFi maturation and real-world asset tokenization
- Pectra and future upgrades improving throughput and UX

## Risks

- **Value accrual uncertainty** — L2 scaling reduces L1 fee revenue; does ETH capture enough value?
- **Competing L1s** — Solana, others capturing transaction volume with faster/cheaper UX
- **Regulatory** — ETH's regulatory status less clear than BTC's in some jurisdictions
- **Weaker monetary premium** — BTC is "digital gold"; ETH doesn't have an equivalent narrative anchor
- **Concentration** — crypto at 37% of portfolio; ETH is a material contributor
