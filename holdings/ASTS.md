# ASTS — AST SpaceMobile

## Thesis

Direct-to-smartphone satellite network — the highest speculation-to-revenue ratio in the [[strategy/thesis-space|Space Economy]] thesis. AT&T, Verizon, Vodafone, and TELUS partnerships. ~$1.2B backlog. BlueBird 7 was supposed to be the next major catalyst, but AST said on April 19, 2026 that the New Glenn launch placed the satellite into a lower-than-planned orbit and it will be de-orbited.

AT&T, Verizon, Vodafone, and TELUS partnerships, ~$1.2B backlog, 488x EV/Revenue.

488x EV/Revenue — priced for perfection. The thesis requires near-flawless execution: AST now needs to recover quickly from the BlueBird 7 loss, commercial service still needs to launch in 2026, and revenue must scale aggressively to grow into the valuation. Any additional launch failure or service delay triggers a violent repricing. Small position size is the only hedge.

If direct-to-device works at scale, ASTS becomes a telecom infrastructure company with global addressable market. That's the multi-bagger case. But the path is narrow.

## Catalysts

- BlueBird 8+ launch timing and management's remediation plan after BlueBird 7
- Commercial service launch in at least one market in 2026
- Additional satellite deployments expanding coverage
- Carrier partnership expansions beyond current agreements

## Risks

- **488x EV/Revenue** — most expensive position in the portfolio by this metric
- **Launch / deployment risk** — BlueBird 7 was lost after reaching a lower-than-planned orbit; another failure would hit confidence hard
- **Execution timeline** — any delay to commercial service challenges the valuation
- **SpaceX Direct-to-Cell** — competitive overhang from Starlink
- **Capital intensity** — building a satellite constellation is enormously expensive
